2. What Agrondo does

Agrondo is an iOS app for mapping fields and keeping farm records: draw field and parcel boundaries on a map and read the area, keep a journal of crops, treatments, operations, harvests and photos, track costs and revenue, get a crop rotation warning, see a weather forecast for your first field, identify a plant from a photo and read a plant care library, set reminders and export reports as PDF and CSV.

6. Important: results and your responsibility

Agrondo is a record-keeping and information tool, not agronomic, legal, financial or safety advice. Areas come from the boundary you draw on the map and are only as exact as that drawing and the map imagery. Plant identification is a best guess from one photo and can be wrong: do not decide on spraying, feeding or eating a plant from it alone, and confirm with an agronomist. The care library is general information for the species, not for your soil or season. The forecast comes from a third party and can be wrong; judge field work on the ground. Nothing in the app recommends a plant protection product.
