1. Who we are

Visto Apps is the brand of Darius Cirjan, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what the Agrondo app (iOS) and the website https://agrondo.com do with personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: iosallapps@gmail.com. Website: vistoapps.com. We answer within one month (art. 12 GDPR). We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

2. At a glance

  • No account. Agrondo does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your fields, parcels, journal entries, photos, costs and reminders stay on your device.
  • One thing sends a photo away: when you tap to identify a plant, the photo goes through our relay on Cloudflare Workers to Pl@ntNet. The relay does not keep it.
  • The forecast sends the centre of your first field to Open-Meteo.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads only with your consent; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland no ad loads before you answer the consent form.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) start only after the consent form has been answered in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Crash reports help us fix bugs.
  • We do not sell personal data for money. Our only server is the small plant relay described below, which keeps one counter per installation and nothing else.

3. Data that stays on your device

Field and parcel boundaries, crops, treatments, operations, harvests, costs and revenue, journal photos, reminders and settings (for example the farm name, area unit and currency) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. There is no sync to iCloud or to any server of ours, and we cannot see them. Reports and the CSV export are created on the device and leave it only through the share sheet, to the place you choose. You delete data in the app item by item (for example Delete Field, Delete Entry); there is no single button that erases everything. Deleting the app removes all of it. Because it is not synced, use your normal iPhone backup if you want it kept.

The app also reads a few settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example which purchase screen to show), a request that carries the installation ID and your IP address and does not depend on the consent form.

5. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature, and works without them. Change them anytime in your device settings.

  • Camera. To take a photo for your field journal or to identify a plant. A photo taken for identification is sent only when you tap to identify it.
  • Photo library. To add pictures to your field journal, or to pick a picture of a plant to identify. Only the pictures you pick are read; the app never browses your library.
  • Location (while using the app). To centre the map on your fields. It is read on the device and is not sent to any service: the forecast uses the centre of the first field you drew, not your position.
  • Notifications. Local reminders scheduled on the device. No push server is involved.
  • Tracking (iOS, App Tracking Transparency). Asked once, after the consent form. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.

6. Consent, ads and tracking

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer it, no ad is requested and no usage statistics are sent. If you refuse personalised ads, you still get limited, non-personalised ads.

On iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes after the consent form. Ads load only once it is answered.

Change your choice anytime: in the app, Settings, Support, Ad Privacy Settings (shown where the consent form applies). On iOS, also Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Buying the paid version removes ads.

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through App Store. The store processes the payment; we receive only a pseudonymous receipt, checked through RevenueCat, that tells the app which purchase you own. We never see your card or account details.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Data held by the services above is kept for the period listed in the table. Our plant relay keeps one counter per installation and month, which deletes itself about 40 days later; it keeps no photo. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (Google, RevenueCat and others marked in the table) process data in the United States or other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing (including to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Because the app has no account, we usually cannot tell which data at Google or RevenueCat is yours. Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and include, if you can, the RevenueCat ID shown in the app or your purchase receipt, and we will forward the request to the provider. You can always delete all data on the device by deleting the app.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work, or to ours: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro. The list of EU authorities: edpb.europa.eu.

11. United States

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • When you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier for ads based on activity in other apps. Some US state laws (for example California) call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time: refuse tracking on iOS (Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), delete or reset the advertising ID on Android, or email us and we will treat it as an opt-out request.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and will not be treated differently for doing so. Categories collected in the last 12 months are the ones in the table above (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location from IP, purchase records). Requests: iosallapps@gmail.com.

12. Children

Agrondo is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child gave us data, write to iosallapps@gmail.com and we will delete what we can reach. Ads in the app are not designed for children.

13. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data on the device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your device to the minimum listed here.

14. This website

https://agrondo.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which records the IP address of visitors in server logs for security. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ads. It stores only your language choice in your browser (local storage).

15. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships. Important changes are also mentioned in the store update notes.
