Draw the boundary. The hectares come back.

Trace a field on the satellite map with your finger. Agrondo returns the area in hectares or square metres. Split a field into parcels and each parcel carries its own figure.

Where it lives

Your records stay on the phone.

Agrondo keeps everything on the device. There is no server holding your yields. Three things reach the network: the satellite imagery, which Apple Maps streams in as you pan, the weather forecast, and a photo you send off to be identified. Your records need none of them.
